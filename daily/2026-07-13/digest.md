# 2026-07-13 — 오늘의 표현

- **collapse (distinct things) into one** — 별개 문제를 하나로 뭉뚱그리다. "There are three distinct things that you're collapsing into one."
- **the deciding detail** — 설계 방향을 확정지은 결정적 한 조각. "That's the deciding detail — and it actually makes your life easier."
- **the single highest-leverage thing** — 투입 대비 효과가 가장 큰 단 하나의 행동. "That's the single highest-leverage thing you could do here."
- **necessary but not sufficient** — 필요조건이지만 충분조건은 아니다. "The code reading the tree is necessary but not sufficient."
- **a judgment call (made unilaterally)** — 정답 없는 재량 판단 — 혼자 내릴 일은 아니다. "That's a judgment call I shouldn't make unilaterally."
- **cheap insurance (against)** — 몇 줄로 큰 사고를 막는 값싼 보험. "The guard is cheap insurance against a partial download."
- **always one alarm behind** — 늘 한 사이클 늦게 도착하는 타이밍 결함. "The download was always one alarm behind."
